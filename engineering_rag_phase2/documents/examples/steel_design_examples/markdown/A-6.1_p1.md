EXAMPLE A-6.1 · AISC 360-22 A6 (stability bracing (Appendix 6)) · §App6 §6.2.2 · Point Stability Bracing of a W-Shape Column

## Engineering Design Report

**Code basis:** ANSI/AISC 360-22, Appendix 6 (Member Stability Bracing), Section 6.2.2 (Point Bracing), per the RAG extract provided.

---

### 1. Design Data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Member | W12x72, ASTM A992/A992M (Fy = 50 ksi, Fu = 65 ksi) |
| Axial loads | D = 105 kips, L = 315 kips |
| Bracing | Lateral point braces at third points; Lbr = 12 ft = 144 in. |
| Column strength at Lc = 12 ft | φcPn = 806 kips (LRFD); Pn/Ωc = 536 kips (ASD) |

### 2. Required Axial Strength, Pr

Using ASCE/SEI 7 load combinations:

- **LRFD:** Pr = Pu = 1.2(105) + 1.6(315) = **630 kips**
- **ASD:** Pr = Pa = 105 + 315 = **420 kips**

Check column adequacy at Lc = 12 ft: 630 kips < 806 kips (LRFD) ✓; 420 kips < 536 kips (ASD) ✓. Per App. 6, Section 6.1, the column may be designed with Lc equal to the distance between braced points when the bracing satisfies Section 6.2.

### 3. Required Brace Strength — Spec. Eq. A-6-3 (Section 6.2.2)

Per Section 6.2.2, the required strength of end and intermediate point braces is:

> Pbr = 0.01 Pr  (Eq. A-6-3)

- **LRFD:** Pbr = 0.01(630) = **6.30 kips**
- **ASD:** Pbr = 0.01(420) = **4.20 kips**

### 4. Required Brace Stiffness — Spec. Eqs. A-6-4a / A-6-4b (Section 6.2.2)

> β_br = (1/φ)(8 Pr / Lbr), φ = 0.75 (LRFD)  (Eq. A-6-4a)
> β_br = Ω(8 Pr / Lbr), Ω = 2.00 (ASD)  (Eq. A-6-4b)

With Lbr = 144 in. (equal adjacent unbraced lengths, so Pr/Lbr is the same on each side):

- **LRFD:** β_br = (1/0.75)(8 × 630 / 144) = (1.333)(35.0) = **46.7 kip/in.**
- **ASD:** β_br = 2.00 × (8 × 420 / 144) = 2.00(23.3) = **46.7 kip/in.**

### 5. Reduced Stiffness Using Maximum Permitted Lc (Section 6.2.2 relaxation)

Section 6.2.2 states: "For intermediate point bracing of an individual column, Lbr in Equations A-6-4a or A-6-4b need not be taken as less than the maximum effective length, Lc, permitted for the column based upon the required axial strength, Pr."

The maximum effective length at which the column just supports Pr is given as Lc = 19.0 ft (LRFD) and 18.9 ft (ASD). Substituting these for Lbr:

- **LRFD:** Lbr = 19.0 ft = 228 in.
  β_br = (1/0.75)(8 × 630 / 228) = (1.333)(22.1) = **29.5 kip/in.**
- **ASD:** Lbr = 18.9 ft = 226.8 in.
  β_br = 2.00 × (8 × 420 / 226.8) = 2.00(14.8) = **29.6 kip/in.**

### 6. Summary of Bracing Requirements

| Quantity | LRFD | ASD | Spec. Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required brace strength, Pbr | 6.30 kips | 4.20 kips | App. 6, §6.2.2, Eq. A-6-3 |
| Required brace stiffness, β_br (Lbr = 12 ft) | 46.7 kip/in. | 46.7 kip/in. | Eq. A-6-4a / A-6-4b |
| Reduced β_br (Lbr = max Lc) | 29.5 kip/in. | 29.6 kip/in. | §6.2.2 Lbr relaxation |

### 7. Conclusion

Each intermediate point brace shall be designed for a required strength of 6.30 kips (LRFD) / 4.20 kips (ASD) per Eq. A-6-3, and a required stiffness of 46.7 kip/in. per Eqs. A-6-4a/b with Lbr = 12 ft. Where the engineer elects to use the Section 6.2.2 provision permitting Lbr to be taken as the maximum effective length Lc at which the column can support Pr (19.0 ft LRFD / 18.9 ft ASD), the required stiffness reduces to 29.5 kip/in. (LRFD) / 29.6 kip/in. (ASD) — a reduction of approximately 37%. Per App. 6, §6.1, the available strength and stiffness of the bracing and its connections shall equal or exceed these required values.
