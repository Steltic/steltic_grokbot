## 4.15.1.15. AcousticMedium

**nDMaterial(*'AcousticMedium'*, *matTag*, *K*, *rho*)**

This command is used to construct an acoustic medium NDMaterial object.

| `matTag` ([int](https://docs.python.org/3/library/functions.html#int)) | integer tag identifying material |
| --- | --- |
| `K` ([float](https://docs.python.org/3/library/functions.html#float)) | bulk module of the acoustic medium |
| `rho` ([float](https://docs.python.org/3/library/functions.html#float)) | mass density of the acoustic medium |
